Buying

Looking for

Homes and plots at very low prices. Homes score higher in a good location (a town with services, "centro", near the beach) and in good condition; they score lower when they need heavy work (ruins, "para recuperar") or are isolated. Urban plots are welcome. Rural plots only count when they are big and cheap:

10 000 m² = 1 hectare. Smaller rural plots are pushed down.

0.50 €/m² = €5 000 per hectare. Dearer land is pushed down; half of it scores best.

Score weights

100% is the normal weight; 0% ignores it, 200% doubles it. Each goal has its own dials, so the price of a home and the price of a plot move separately. Only bonuses and penalties move: the rules (too hot by 2090, occupied, plot too small…) stay rules. Saved with the other settings; the list re-scores at once.

Budget & what to show

One budget per goal. A scan keeps anything under the highest of the three, so nothing is thrown away; each tab, its alerts and its part of the report then show only what fits its own budget. A home bought to let or resell is usually dearer than the cheapest house, and land is priced by the hectare.

The most one purchase may take: the price, the taxes and fees, and the low end of repairs. A listing above it is hidden (a ☆ one stays). An offer above it asks for a reason. 0 means not set, and offers stay blocked until you set it.

0 is an ordinary listing and 100 is the best, so the ones worth a look use the whole scale.

Your details (on every letter)

Bid cap

The calculator on Listings and Offers works from the local median price (or your land target), the taxes and fees, the renovation bands, a risk reserve and the climate grade. It shows a recommended bid and an absolute maximum, with a waterfall of how it got there. It never changes an amount you type; when you send or log a different figure, the difference is kept with the offer. It is an estimate from local medians, not a valuation.

Due-diligence checklist

Each offer on the Offers page has a checklist for its country and way of selling. Ticked items below are blocking: until you mark them verified or not applicable, sending or logging an offer asks why you are going ahead, and your reason is kept with the offer. "Verified" means you checked it; the checklist is your own record, not legal advice.

Location check

Most sales place a property only at its town. Then the climate, flood, water and distance checks describe the town, not the property. Verify a position in the listing's ⓘ panel (coordinates, an address, or a Spanish cadastral reference). For an offer on a listing placed only at its municipality or not at all, the Offers page can warn you, or also ask your reason before the offer is sent or logged. The reason is kept with the offer. Information requests are never held back.

Climate

Each listing's Climate panel grades the place from the climate maps (heat, floods, water, fire). With the guardrail on, the AI check on Offers lowers its suggested bid and maximum for a caution or poor grade (more for poor, and it says by how much), only where the position is exact. It never changes an amount you type. The grade is a planning indicator, not a survey or due diligence.

Land stewardship

Rural listings get a non-binding Climate & Land Stewardship Plan on Listings → ⓘ (risks, opportunities, 90-day and 3-year actions, questions for local advisers). It uses the climate maps and location confidence only; it never invents protected areas, water rights or planting permission. Costs are planning allowances. Export as Markdown, PDF or Word.

Alerts

Automatic scanning

Telegram

A cut on something on Offers is always sent, whatever it scores.

E-mail

The account the app sends from: alerts, and letters sent with Send by e-mail on Offers (a copy of each letter comes back to this account). Gmail: smtp.gmail.com, port 587, and an app password.

Information requests

After a scan, for strong sales where the agente, court or lawyer's e-mail is known, the app prepares the information request (is the sale still on, deadline, deposit, charges, occupancy, visits) and offers it on Telegram with Send, Show letter and Skip. Nothing is sent until you tap Send; sent requests appear under Offers → Sent. Needs Telegram, the e-mail account and your name and e-mail under Buying.

Accounts

Site logins

Some sites show their best sales only to account holders (the Portal das Finanças' seized goods). With your account here the scan signs in for you. The password is encrypted by Windows for your user on this PC and never shown again. If a site asks for an SMS code, shows a CAPTCHA or refuses the password, the app stops trying for a day and tells you on Telegram.

Street View

Each listing's panel links to Google Street View at the property. To see Street View inside the panel, paste a Google Maps Embed API key (free: Google Cloud console → APIs → "Maps Embed API" → Credentials). It stays on this PC.

Photo check

After each scan, a vision model looks at the photos of the best homes (a few each scan, each once) and says whether the building looks sound, needs work or is a ruin. It is used when the listing's text says nothing about the condition. Open model (Ollama): free and private, runs on this PC — install Ollama from ollama.com and run ollama pull qwen2.5vl:3b; without a graphics card a photo takes about a minute. Claude: faster, needs an Anthropic API key (it stays on this PC; the AI check on Offers uses it too).

Advanced

Backup

Everything you have decided — Offers picks, sent letters, outcomes, the whole history — is in auctions.db on this PC. Give the app a folder that is not on this PC (a OneDrive or Dropbox folder, another drive, a memory stick) and it copies the database there once a day, keeping the last few. To go back to a copy: close the app, and rename the copy to auctions.db in the app's folder.

Updates

New versions are published on GitHub (the master branch). The app fetches them when it starts, backs up your database to backups/, installs any new packages and restarts itself. Your listings, offers and settings are never touched.
